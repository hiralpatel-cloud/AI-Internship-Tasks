Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the simulation of human intelligence by machines.

Machine Learning is a subset of AI that enables computers to learn from data.

Deep Learning is a subset of Machine Learning that uses neural networks.

Python is one of the most popular programming languages for AI development.

Generative AI creates new content such as text, images, music, and code.

Large Language Models (LLMs) understand and generate human language.

Transformers are deep learning architectures used in modern LLMs.

Embeddings convert text into numerical vectors.

FAISS is a library used for similarity search over vector embeddings.

RAG stands for Retrieval-Augmented Generation.
